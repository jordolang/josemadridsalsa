Hi there, friend 👋

For more than three decades, we've handcrafted every batch of José Madrid Salsa in small kettles right here in Zanesville, Ohio — real ingredients, old-family recipes, and no shortcuts. We're writing to let you know about a change we've worked hard to put off for as long as we could.

Beginning September 1, 2026, every jar sold through our fundraising programs will be $10. Between now and then, nothing changes — the current price still stands on every fundraiser that runs before that date.

✅ Shopping on our website? Nothing changes.

This update applies to our fundraising platforms only. Everyday orders at josemadridsalsa.com keep their current pricing — the price increase does not affect them.

Why the change?
🌱 Real ingredients cost more.
The tomatoes, peppers, and fresh produce we refuse to compromise on have climbed steadily in price.
👐 Still made by hand.
Small-batch, kettle-cooked salsa takes real people and real time — and we're proud to keep it that way.
🫙 Same jar, same flavor.
The recipe, the size, and the quality you love are exactly the same. Only the price is changing.

🎁 Planning a fundraiser? Lock in today's price

Every fundraiser that runs before September 1, 2026 keeps today's price. If you've been thinking about a salsa fundraiser for your team, school, or group, now's the time to get it on the calendar.
