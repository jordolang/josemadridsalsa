## Payment problems

| Message you see | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| **Card declined** / **Insufficient funds** | Try another card or payment method, or call your bank. |
| **Expired card** | Check the expiration date, or use a different card. |
| **Incorrect card number or CVC** | Re-enter the card number and the 3–4 digit security code carefully. |
| **Flagged as potentially fraudulent** | Your bank blocked the charge as a precaution. Call the number on the back of your card to approve it, then try again. |
| **PayPal / Venmo / Cash App options are missing** | You have a discount code or gift certificate applied. Pay with a card or Link, or remove the code. |

<Callout title="Was I charged twice?">
If a payment failed and you retried, the first attempt may show as a **pending** charge that drops off on its own within a few days. If you see two *completed* charges, [contact us](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/contact) and we'll refund the extra.
</Callout>

## Sign-in problems

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title='"Invalid email or password"'>
    Check for typos and Caps Lock. If you originally signed up with Google, Apple, Facebook, or GitHub, use that button instead. You can always [reset your password](/help/account#forgot-your-password).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='"Email already associated with another account"'>
    You've used this email with a different sign-in method before. Sign in the way you did the first time (for example, with Google instead of a password).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm locked out after too many attempts">
    For security, sign-in pauses after 5 wrong attempts. Wait **15 minutes**, then try again, or reset your password.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I keep getting sent back to the sign-in page">
    Your browser may be blocking cookies. Make sure cookies are allowed for josemadridsalsa.com, close any private/incognito window, and try again. Clearing cookies for the site often fixes it too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='"Account created, but automatic sign in failed"'>
    Your account was created! Just go to [Sign in](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/auth/signin) and sign in normally.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Emails aren't arriving

Order confirmations, shipping notices, and password resets come from our store. If you don't see one:

1. **Check spam, junk, and Promotions tabs.**
2. Search your inbox for "Jose Madrid".
3. Make sure you used the right email at checkout. You can confirm it in **Account → Orders**.
4. Add our address to your contacts so future emails land in your inbox.

Still nothing after an hour? [Contact us](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/contact) and we'll resend it.

## Cart or website acting strange

- **Refresh the page.** Many glitches fix themselves.
- **Try another browser** or turn off ad blockers for our site. Some block the payment form.
- **Clear your cart** and add items again if quantities or prices look wrong.
- Make sure your browser is up to date.

## Reporting a bug

Found something broken? We'd love to hear about it. [Send us a message](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/contact) and include:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead (a screenshot helps a lot!)
- Your device and browser (for example, "iPhone, Safari")
- Your order number, if it's about an order

Thank you for helping us make the store better! 🌶️
