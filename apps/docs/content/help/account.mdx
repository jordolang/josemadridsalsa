## Creating an account

[Sign up](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/auth/signup) with your email and a password (at least **8 characters**), or use **Continue with Google, Apple, Facebook, or GitHub**.

With an account you can:

- See your **order history** and track shipments
- Save **shipping addresses** for faster checkout
- Keep a **wishlist** of flavors to try
- Earn **rewards** on your purchases

## Signing in

Go to [Sign in](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/auth/signin). You'll stay signed in on that device for up to 30 days.

<Callout type="warn" title="Use the same sign-in method every time">
If you created your account with **Google** (or Apple, Facebook, GitHub), keep signing in with that button. Typing the same email with a password won't work, and signing in with a *different* social button that uses the same email may show an "already associated with another account" error.
</Callout>

## Forgot your password?

1. Go to [Forgot password](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/auth/forgot-password) and enter your email.
2. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for the reset link.
3. Click it **within 1 hour** and choose a new password.

You'll always see "If an account exists, a link has been sent" — that's for your privacy, so nobody can check which emails have accounts. If no email arrives, you may have signed up with a different email or with a social button (see above).

## Your account pages

| Page | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Your dashboard and recent orders |
| **Orders** | Every order, its status, and tracking |
| **Rewards** | Your loyalty points |
| **Settings** | Your name, saved addresses, and email preferences |
| **Wishlist** | Flavors you've saved for later |

## Deleting your account and data

See [Delete my data](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/deletemydata). In short: email us with the subject **"Delete My Data"**, tell us which sign-in method you used, and we'll finish within 30 days. Records of past orders may be kept.
