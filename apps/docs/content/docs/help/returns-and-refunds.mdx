title: Returns and refunds
description: How to return unopened jars within 14 days and how refunds are paid.
---

## Can I return my order?

Yes — **unopened jars** can be returned within **14 days** of receiving your order.
Opened, used or unsealed jars can't be refunded, and requests after 14 days can't be accepted.

## How do I start a return?

Email [mike@josemadridsalsa.com](mailto:mike@josemadridsalsa.com) with your order number
and we'll send return instructions.

## Who pays return shipping?

You do. Return shipping and handling is non-refundable, and we recommend a trackable
method — we can't refund items that never reach us.

## When do I get my refund?

Once we receive and inspect the unopened jars, we refund the original payment method.
Return shipping costs aren't included in the refund.

Full policy: [Return Policy](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/returns).
