# Email Marketing

The email marketing system supports campaign sending, automation workflows, template management, suppression lists, and rate-limited delivery. It handles both marketing campaigns and transactional emails (order confirmation, shipping notifications, etc.).

## Email Types

The system handles these email categories:

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `order-confirmation` | Sent after successful payment |
| `shipping-notification` | Sent when order ships with tracking |
| `delivery-confirmation` | Sent on delivery with feedback link |
| `contact-form` | Internal notification for contact submissions |
| `welcome` | New user registration |
| `newsletter` | Marketing newsletter |
| `abandoned-cart` | Cart recovery email |
| `fundraiser-followup` | Fundraiser campaign follow-up |

## Automation Engine

The automation engine (`lib/email/automation-engine.ts`) processes trigger-based email workflows. Supported triggers:

```ts
type AutomationTriggerType =
  | 'USER_REGISTERED'
  | 'ORDER_PLACED'
  | 'ORDER_SHIPPED'
  | 'ORDER_DELIVERED'
  | 'ABANDONED_CART'
  | 'LOYALTY_POINTS_EARNED'
  | 'LOYALTY_TIER_UPGRADE'
  | 'SUBSCRIPTION_CREATED'
  | 'BIRTHDAY'
  | 'REENGAGEMENT'
  | 'LOW_STOCK'
  | 'CUSTOM'
```

### Enrollment Flow

<Steps>
### Trigger Fires

An event occurs (e.g., `ORDER_PLACED`) and calls `enrollInAutomation()`.

### Find Matching Automations

The engine queries all active automations for the trigger type.

### Create Enrollment

For each matching automation, an `AutomationEnrollment` record is created (or reactivated if previously completed). The first step's delay is calculated.

### Process Steps

A cron job calls `processDueAutomationSteps()` to send emails for enrollments whose `nextStepAt` has passed. After sending, it advances to the next step or marks the enrollment as completed.
</Steps>

### Delay Support

Each automation step has a configurable `delayHours` property. The engine calculates `nextStepAt` by adding the delay to the current time.

## Campaign Sender

The `runCampaignSender()` function (`lib/email/campaign-sender.ts`) processes lead-generation email campaigns:

1. Loads the campaign and its template
2. Fetches leads with status `CONTACT_FOUND` and valid email
3. For each lead:
   - Substitutes template variables (`{{school_name}}`, `{{contact_name}}`, `{{sport}}`, etc.)
   - Sends the email via the email sender
   - Updates lead status to `EMAIL_SENT` or `EMAIL_FAILED`
   - Emits progress events via the scraper event bus

## Audience Check Before Sending

Every campaign's first send needs a reviewed audience (`lib/email/audience-check.ts`). **Launch
Campaign** on `/admin/email-campaigns/[id]` opens a check that shows:

- the recipient count and the list it came from,
- a sample of up to 10 recipients, named people first,
- how many recipients have an open order and how many do not,
- **Only send to people with an open order**, which removes recipients without one. It is on by
  default for campaigns on the Open Orders list, and for campaigns whose name or subject reads
  like a shipping or order update (ship, deliver, tracking, delay, "your order").

Confirming is recorded in the audit log (`email_campaign.audience_confirmed`) with the exact
recipient count approved. The Launch button, `POST /api/admin/campaigns/[id]/send` and the
scheduled-send cron all refuse a campaign that has not started and has no confirmation for its
current count. A scheduled campaign without one stays `SCHEDULED` and shows **Review Audience** on
its page. Resuming a paused campaign does not ask again.

## Open Orders List

The **Open Orders** mailing list (fixed id `open-orders`) holds everyone whose order has not shipped
yet (`lib/email/open-orders.ts`). It is never edited by hand: each refresh purges every entry and
rebuilds it from the site's own orders, including fundraiser and BigCommerce-mirrored orders.

An order is open when all of these hold:

- payment is `PAID` or `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED`,
- status is `PENDING`, `CONFIRMED` or `PROCESSING`,
- fulfillment is `UNFULFILLED` or `PARTIALLY_FULFILLED`,
- the sales channel is not `POS` or `EVENT` (taken home at the table).

Each person appears once, with the email from their account or guest checkout and the shipping
name. Tags carry the sales channel, and `customFields.orderNumbers` lists their open orders.
People who opted out (email suppressions, unsubscribe-all preferences, or a non-mailable
customer `emailStatus`) are kept as `UNSUBSCRIBED`, `BOUNCED` or `COMPLAINED` rows so campaigns
skip them.

The list refreshes when you click **Refresh Open Orders List** on `/admin/communications/lists`
(which also creates it the first time), and automatically whenever a campaign is created on it,
from the campaign page or the desktop admin.

## Suppression Lists

The `checkSuppression()` function (`lib/email/suppression.ts`) checks whether an email address should be excluded from sending (unsubscribed, bounced, or complained).

## Rate Limiting

The `lib/email/rate-limit.ts` module enforces sending rate limits to prevent provider throttling and maintain deliverability.
