# Self-Order Kiosk

Customers build their own order on a touch screen, pay on a Square Terminal, and
take a printed receipt. It has two parts:

| Part | Where | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Kiosk screens | `/kiosk` in the storefront (`app/kiosk`, `components/kiosk`) | Splash screen, menu, order, payment, confirmation |
| Android app | `apps/android-kiosk` | Locks the tablet to `/kiosk`, drives the USB receipt printer, passes the barcode scanner through |

Because the screens are a web page, a design change ships with a normal deploy; the
tablet picks it up on its next reload without reinstalling the app.

## Hardware

- An Android tablet (Android 8 or later) in a floor or counter stand.
- A **Square Terminal**: the customer taps, inserts or swipes there.
- An **80mm USB ESC/POS receipt printer** (Epson TM-T20/T88, Rongta, Munbyn and the
  like) on the tablet's USB port, through an OTG adapter or hub.
- Optionally, a **USB barcode scanner**. Scanners act as keyboards; a scan adds that
  jar to the order.

## How a sale works

1. **Splash screen.** A loop of the burning-logo opening from the website hero, the
   price chart, and *Touch to start*.
2. **Menu.** Tiles come from `lib/kiosk/catalog.ts` (photo, label color, heat, filter
   group, UPC). `GET /api/kiosk/catalog` says which flavors this kiosk can sell and
   which are sold out.
3. **Pay.** The tablet sends `POST /api/kiosk/checkout` with flavor keys and
   quantities, **never prices**. The server prices the cart (below), reserves stock,
   creates a `KIOSK-YYYYMMDD-NNNN` order, and sends the total to the Square Terminal.
4. **Waiting on the card.** The tablet polls `GET /api/kiosk/checkout/{checkoutId}`
   every 2 seconds. A completed checkout marks the order paid, records the `Payment`,
   deducts stock and emits `payment.completed`. *Cancel payment* calls
   `POST /api/kiosk/checkout/{checkoutId}/cancel`; if the card went through just
   before the cancel, the sale is kept.
5. **Confirmation.** The receipt prints, and the kiosk returns to the splash screen
   after 10 seconds.

A menu or cart left untouched for 90 seconds clears and returns to the splash screen.

The kiosk and the staff [POS](/docs/features/pos) share one Square Terminal
implementation, `lib/pos/terminal-checkout.ts`, so a kiosk sale is recorded exactly
like a counter sale (`salesChannel: POS`, `paymentChannel: POS`). The kiosk's orders
are the ones numbered `KIOSK-…`, and their admin notes name the deal applied.

## Pricing

`lib/kiosk/pricing.ts` is the booth sign as code:

| Jars | Price |
|---|---|
| 1 | $10 |
| 3 | $25 |
| 4 | $32 |
| 5 + a bag of chips (*Show Special*) | $40 |
| 12 (case) | $80 |

Flavors mix and match, and any count is charged the **cheapest combination** of
bundles. Ties go to the combination with more free chips. For example, 7 jars are a
3-jar deal plus a 4-jar deal ($57), and 10 jars are two Show Specials ($80, two bags of
chips). The order stores every jar at $10 and the bundle savings as `discountAmount`.

Kiosk sales carry **no sales tax**: Ohio exempts grocery food such as salsa.

## Matching flavors to products

Each kiosk flavor sells one `Product`. `matchKioskProducts` pairs them by
`Product.barcode` (the UPC), confirmed by name. A product without a barcode is matched
by name alone ("Mango Habanero Salsa" matches "Mango Habanero"). A flavor with no
matching active product is hidden from the menu and listed in the catalog response's
`unmatched` array, which the tablet logs to its console.

Scanning: `093662452911` is **Cherry Mild** (an older `upc.txt` also listed it for Green
Apple, which was wrong). **Blueberry Mild** is `093662453017` and **Cherry Hot** is
`093662453000`. **Green Apple** is retired and has no barcode, so it can't be scanned. A
code that matches more than one flavor asks the customer to tap their jar.

## Pairing a tablet

The kiosk API accepts either a **device token** or a logged-in staff session with
`orders:write` (so staff can test `/kiosk` in a browser).

1. Generate a token of at least 24 characters, for example `openssl rand -hex 24`.
2. Add it to `KIOSK_DEVICE_TOKENS` in Vercel. Separate several tablets' tokens with
   commas, so one can be revoked without the others.
3. Enter the same token in the Android app's setup screen.

A token shorter than 24 characters is ignored. A tablet whose token is missing or
wrong shows *"Staff: This kiosk is not paired"* on the splash screen.

## The Android app

`apps/android-kiosk` is a Kotlin app (`net.josemadrid.kiosk`, Android 8+) that:

- shows `/kiosk` full screen and blocks navigation off the kiosk's origin;
- locks the tablet to itself (`startLockTask`, fully locked when it is device owner),
  starts at boot, and acts as the Home app;
- exposes `window.JMKiosk` to the page: `getDeviceToken()`, `getAppVersion()`,
  `printerStatus()` and `printReceipt(json)` (`lib/kiosk/bridge.ts` is the web side);
- prints receipts to the first USB printer it finds (ESC/POS, 48 columns, partial cut);
- leaves the barcode scanner's keystrokes to the page.

**Build** (needs JDK 17 and the Android SDK):

```bash
cd apps/android-kiosk
JAVA_HOME=/opt/homebrew/opt/openjdk@17/libexec/openjdk.jdk/Contents/Home ./gradlew assembleDebug testDebugUnitTest
```

**Install on a factory-reset tablet** (before adding a Google account, which device
owner requires):

```bash
adb install app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
adb shell dpm set-device-owner net.josemadrid.kiosk/.KioskDeviceAdminReceiver
```

Open the app, enter the kiosk URL (`https://www.josemadrid.net/kiosk`), the device
token and a staff PIN, and choose it as the Home app. Plug the printer in and accept
the USB permission prompt once.

**Staff menu:** press and hold the top-right corner for 3 seconds, then enter the PIN.
The menu has Reload, Printer test, Settings and Exit kiosk. If the app is the Home app,
Android reopens it after *Exit kiosk*; change the Home app in Settings first. As device
owner, it can be removed only with `adb shell dpm remove-active-admin` or a factory reset.

<Callout type="warn">
Only a debug build exists, and nothing has been tried on a physical tablet, printer,
scanner or Square Terminal yet. Run a full test sale (`SQUARE_SANDBOX` unset, against a
sandbox Terminal) before the first show.
</Callout>

## Configuration

| Variable | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `KIOSK_DEVICE_TOKENS` | Comma-separated device tokens, one per paired tablet |
| `SQUARE_ACCESS_TOKEN` | Square API access (shared with the POS) |
| `SQUARE_TERMINAL_DEVICE_ID` | The Square Terminal the kiosk charges |
| `SQUARE_SANDBOX` | `false` for real charges; anything else uses the Square sandbox |
