## How to order

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Pick your flavors
    Browse [all salsas](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/products) and add the ones you want to your cart. Not sure how hot to go? Check the [heat guide](/help/products#heat-levels).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check out
    Open your cart and click **Checkout**. You can sign in or check out as a guest — no account required.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter your shipping address
    Shipping and sales tax are calculated from your address, so you'll see the full total before you pay.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pay and watch your inbox
    You'll get an order confirmation email right away, and another email with tracking once your order ships.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment methods

We accept:

- **Credit and debit cards** — Visa, Mastercard, and American Express
- **Link** — Stripe's one-click saved checkout
- **PayPal**
- **Venmo**
- **Cash App Pay**

Some devices also show an express checkout button at the top of the checkout page.

<Callout type="warn" title="Using a discount code or gift certificate?">
When a discount code or gift certificate is applied, **PayPal, Venmo, and Cash App Pay are turned off** for that order. Pay with a card or Link instead. If you'd rather use PayPal, remove the code first.
</Callout>

## Common questions

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an account to order?">
    No. Guest checkout works fine. An account lets you see your order history, save addresses, keep a wishlist, and earn rewards. See [Your Account](/help/account).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do you charge sales tax?">
    Yes, where required. Tax is calculated automatically from your shipping address and shown before you pay. Approved wholesale resale accounts are tax-exempt; see [wholesale](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/wholesale).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I enter a discount code?">
    On the checkout page, look for the discount / gift certificate field in the order summary. Apply the code before choosing a payment method.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change or cancel my order?">
    Contact us as soon as possible. We may be able to cancel or change an order **before it ships**. Once it's shipped, the address can't be changed. All sales are final after shipping; see [our policy](/help/returns#our-return--refund-policy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is my order number?">
    It's in your confirmation email and starts with `JMS-`. If you have an account, it's also under **Account → Orders**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I buy in bulk for my store or restaurant?">
    Yes! Visit our [wholesale page](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/wholesale).
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

Payment didn't go through? See [Payment problems](/help/troubleshooting#payment-problems).
