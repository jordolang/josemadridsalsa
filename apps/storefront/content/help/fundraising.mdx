Selling salsa is an easy fundraiser: people actually *want* to buy it, and your group keeps a share of every jar sold. There are two ways to run one.

<Tabs items={['Online fundraiser', 'Order-form fundraiser']}>
  <Tab value="Online fundraiser">
    Best for groups whose supporters are spread out, or anyone who doesn't want to handle cash and deliveries.

    <Steps>
      <Step>
        ### Sign up your group
        Register on the [fundraising site](https://fundraising.josemadridsalsa.com/sign-up).
      </Step>
      <Step>
        ### Share your link
        Supporters shop online and choose your **group and seller name** at checkout so the sale is credited to you.
      </Step>
      <Step>
        ### We ship directly to supporters
        Each order ships straight to the buyer for a flat shipping rate. No sorting boxes in the gym!
      </Step>
      <Step>
        ### Get paid
        When your fundraiser ends, we mail your group a check for **$5 for every jar sold**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Order-form fundraiser">
    The classic approach: sellers collect orders on paper forms from friends, family, and coworkers.

    <Steps>
      <Step>
        ### Download a kit
        Pick a kit with **25, 16, or 9 flavors** from the [fundraising site](https://fundraising.josemadridsalsa.com). Each kit includes an order form, a tracking sheet, a flavor handout, a launch guide, and salsa facts.
      </Step>
      <Step>
        ### Sell for 2–3 weeks
        That's the sweet spot. Long enough to reach everyone, short enough to keep the energy up.
      </Step>
      <Step>
        ### Submit one combined order
        Add up all your forms and submit a single order online. Pay by card online, by card over the phone, or by mailed check.
      </Step>
      <Step>
        ### Deliver the salsa
        Your group pays **$5 per jar** and sells each jar for $10, keeping the other $5. Orders ship within 10 days of payment, usually within a week.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How much does my group earn?

It's simple: every jar sells for **$10**. **Your group keeps $5**, and $5 goes to Jose Madrid Salsa.

| Jars sold | Your group earns |
| --- | --- |
| 50 | $250 |
| 100 | $500 |
| 250 | $1,250 |

<Callout title="Free shipping on big orders">
Fundraiser orders of **96 jars or more ship free**.
</Callout>

## The Battle Arena

Want to make it competitive? Online fundraisers can join the **Battle Arena**, a monthly leaderboard where every sale powers up the team that earned it. Each season runs one calendar month, and team captains can customize their team page and track sales from the fundraiser portal.
