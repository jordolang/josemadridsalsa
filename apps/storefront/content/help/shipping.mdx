## When will my order ship?

Every jar is packed by hand. Most orders arrive within **5–7 business days**. Some orders need a handling time of **3–5 business days** before they ship, so allow for that too. During peak seasons, holidays, or periods of heavy online order volume, orders may be delayed by up to **14 days** before they ship.

Here's what to expect:

1. **Order confirmation email** — sent right after you pay.
2. **Shipping email with tracking** — sent when your package leaves us.
3. **Tracking goes live** — it can take **24–48 hours** after the shipping email before the carrier shows updates. That's normal!

## How much is shipping?

Your shipping cost is shown at checkout **before you pay**, so there are no surprises.

<Callout title="Is there free shipping?">
Free shipping is only available on:

- **Fundraiser orders of 96 jars or more**
- **Distributor or wholesale accounts** with negotiated shipping terms

All other orders pay the shipping cost shown at checkout.
</Callout>

We ship with **USPS, UPS, and FedEx**. If you're shipping to a **PO Box**, we'll use USPS.

## Where do you ship?

We currently ship to addresses in the **United States only**, including Alaska, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico (rates to these areas are higher). We aren't able to ship internationally at this time.

<Callout title="Perishable items">
A few items may ship with ice packs and are usually sent early in the week so they don't sit in a warehouse over a weekend. **Refrigerate these as soon as they arrive.**
</Callout>

## Tracking your package

- Click the tracking link in your shipping email, **or**
- Sign in and go to **Account → Orders**, then open the order.

## Order status meanings

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | We've received your order and are confirming payment. |
| **Confirmed** | Payment went through and your order is in line to be packed. |
| **Processing** | We're packing your salsa. |
| **Shipped** | It's on its way! Tracking is in your shipping email. |
| **Delivered** | The carrier marked it delivered. |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled and won't ship. |
| **Refunded** | A refund approved by our staff has been sent to your original payment method. |

## Common questions

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="My tracking hasn't updated in days">
    Give it 48 hours after the shipping email. If it still hasn't moved after a few business days, [contact us](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/contact) with your order number and we'll look into it with the carrier.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says delivered, but I don't have it">
    Check with neighbors, building mailrooms, and around your door. If it doesn't turn up within a day, contact us. We'll investigate with the carrier and work with you on a replacement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I entered the wrong address">
    Contact us right away. We can fix it **before the order ships**, but not after.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My package arrived damaged">
    Sorry about that! See [Damaged or broken jars](/help/returns#damaged-or-broken-jars).
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
