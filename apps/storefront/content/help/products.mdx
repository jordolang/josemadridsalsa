## Heat levels

Every salsa falls into one of five families:

| Heat level | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| **Mild** | All flavor, gentle warmth. Great for kids and first-timers. |
| **Medium** | A noticeable kick that builds a little. |
| **Hot** | Real heat for regular spicy-food fans. |
| **Extra Hot** | For serious chileheads. Proceed with chips in hand. |
| **Fruit** | Sweet-and-spicy fruit salsas. |

Each flavor also has a **0–10 heat rating** on its product page. Compare flavors side by side on the [heat index](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/heat-index).

## Dietary info

<Callout title="Gluten-free">
**Every flavor is gluten-free except Chipotle Con Queso.**
</Callout>

Full ingredient lists are on each product's page and on the jar label. If you have a serious allergy, always check the label on the jar you receive.

## Storage

- **Perishable items** may ship with ice packs. Refrigerate them as soon as they arrive.
- Once a jar is opened, keep it in the refrigerator. Check the jar label for storage guidance.

## Where to buy in stores
