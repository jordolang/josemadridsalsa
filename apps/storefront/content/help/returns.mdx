## Broken or damaged jars

Glass and shipping don't always get along. If a jar arrives broken, **we'll send you a replacement**. Here's how:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Take photos
    Photograph the broken jar(s) and the shipping box as soon as you open it.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Email us
    Send the photos and your order number (it starts with `JMS-`) to [mike@josemadridsalsa.com](mailto:mike@josemadridsalsa.com).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Get your replacement
    Once we've confirmed the damage, we'll ship replacement jars for the broken ones.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If we sent you the **wrong flavor**, contact us the same way and we'll make it right.

## Our return & refund policy

<Callout type="warn" title="All sales are final">
Because our salsa is a food product, **we don't accept returns or give refunds** unless a member of the Jose Madrid Salsa staff explicitly approves it. Broken jars are covered by replacement, as described above.
</Callout>

If you think your situation needs a return or refund, [contact us](https://www.josemadridsalsa.com/contact) **before sending anything back**. Packages returned without staff approval can't be accepted.

If a staff member approves a refund, it goes back to your **original payment method**. Your bank may take a few business days to show it.

**Gift certificates are never refundable.**

## Cancelling an order

Contact us as soon as possible. We may be able to cancel an order **if it hasn't shipped yet**, but once it's packed and on its way it can't be cancelled.
