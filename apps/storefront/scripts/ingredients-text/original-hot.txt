__ The story of José Madrid
Back in the days, Lincoln County, New Mexico made its
share of legends. Like the adventures of Billy the Kid and
Sheriff Pat Garret. And like the salsa of José Madrid,
José’s legend started with the peppers and tomatoes he
grew in his garden. And the magnificent salsa he made
from them. Salsa so delightfully tangy and rich, people
en route from Texas to Arizona would go out of their way
across the desert just for a taste.

Now José’s grandchildren present these classic tastes
for you to enjoy. Four kinds of New Mexico peppers...
rich, sweet tomatoes, blended info a hearty, tangy salsa
